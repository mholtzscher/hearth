      The six rules worth carrying away
      
          
      The implementation has plenty of wire detail. These are the choices that shape its behavior under retries, concurrent health changes, takeover, and shutdown.
      
        
      
        
      
          
      01
      Claim identity survives retries
      prepareRequest runs before the claim loop. A lost reply does not create a second logical claim.
      
          
      02
      Health is a latest-value mailbox
      Writers replace desiredHealth and increment a generation. They do not enqueue one heartbeat per call.
      
          
      03
      One goroutine sends heartbeats
      The heartbeat loop serializes transport, acknowledgements, and immediate follow-up when a newer generation appears.
      
          
      04
      Acknowledgement has a generation
      SetHealth returns only after ackedGeneration reaches the generation written by that call.
      
          
      05
      Fencing outranks ordinary closure
      markFenced always writes ErrRuntimeFenced, cancels lifecycle work, wakes waiters, and closes NATS.
      
          
      06
      Release is best effort and bounded
      Close stops heartbeat work first, then gives release five seconds. Lease expiry remains the fallback.
