# Motion

Motion explains a change: it acknowledges an input, shows where something went, reports an outcome, or marks newer data. Every motion uses one of the patterns below with the shared timing tokens. `DESIGN.md` (Motion) holds the full rules.

Stories that show motion in a component or layout are tagged `motion` and named `Motion · <what moves>`. Filter the sidebar by the `motion` tag to see them all, or use [Where it moves](#where-it-moves).

## Timing

Every transition uses `ease-out-expo`; only the pending pulse uses `ease-pulse`. An exit takes the next shorter duration than its entrance. A bare `transition-*` utility defaults to `duration-feedback` on `ease-out-expo`. Values are read from the live CSS.

## Patterns

Each pattern names its reduced-motion behavior. Replay a pattern with its button.

### Arrive

An element entering a sequence fades in while rising 8px at `duration-state`. A block inside an arriving group rises 4px, one feedback step later. Reduced motion: fade.

### Resolve

Text that arrives in pieces fades in from a 3px blur, piece by piece, at `duration-layout`. Reduced motion: fade.

### Grow

A disclosure opens from zero height while fading in, at `duration-layout`; closing reverses it. Reduced motion: fade.

### Slide

A panel fades in while moving 12–16px from the edge it opens from: the canvas at `duration-layout`, drawers at `duration-state`. Reduced motion: none.

### Pending

While an outcome is awaited, a dot or icon breathes between full and 40% opacity on the 1.6-second pending pulse, or a dashed icon turns once every 8 seconds. Reduced motion: none.

<Canvas of={Motion.Pending} sourceState="none" />

### Confirm

An icon swap at `duration-feedback`: the old icon shrinks to 50% and fades, and the new icon grows from 50% and a 2px blur, or a check draws its stroke. Reduced motion: fade.

### Ignite

A line spreads from its center to full width at `duration-layout`. The afterglow then fades it over 10 seconds. Reduced motion: none; the afterglow lights at full width.

### Give

A whole-card press target shrinks to 98.5% while pressed, at `duration-feedback`. Reduced motion: none.

<Canvas of={Motion.Give} sourceState="none" />

### Refuse

A control shakes 3px sideways, one and a half times, at `duration-layout`. Reduced motion: none.

## Reduced motion

Under a reduced-motion preference, `index.css` removes every animation and transition, except on elements marked `data-reduced-motion="fade"`. A marked element keeps only opacity and color transitions at feedback or state duration, declared with `motion-reduce:` utilities. Movement, scaling, blur, breathing, rolling, and shaking stop. The workspace is never marked: the canvas and drawers change at once.
