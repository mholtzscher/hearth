Decisions this mockup should answer

Every toggle is independent, so any combination can be compared.

1. Hourly: scrollable strip or chart? (Chart labels every 2, 3 or 6 hours depending on hours shown.)

2. Hours and days shown: should the card definition choose these, or should the horizon be fixed?

3. Precipitation chance: always, at 10% or more, or at 20% or more? Hidden values keep their space so rows stay aligned; chart bars follow the same threshold.

4. Icons: neutral ink (DESIGN.md default) or lightly tinted (pale gold sun, dusk-blue precipitation)?

5. Past periods are dropped at render time. When none remain, the card says so instead of showing an old forecast.

6. Footer uses the existing "Observed 14m ago" wording; Source shows issue time, timezone and Entity ID.
