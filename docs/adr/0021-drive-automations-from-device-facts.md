# Drive Automations from age-bounded durable Device Facts

This decision amends ADR 0020's statement that Core creates no Device Fact consumer: the Device Fact publishing foundation still creates none, while the separately owned Automations module creates one for its own recovery policy. Fact-driven Automations consume accepted Observation and Entity Event facts through that named durable consumer rather than reading devices tables or subscribing to Adapter input. The consumer starts at the stream's current tail only when first created, resumes its acknowledgement floor afterward, and records a per-Automation `stale_fact` Skip instead of executing a matching Fact more than 30 seconds after Core's `emitted_at`; an indefinitely retained matched-Fact receipt makes admission idempotent on `(fact_id, automation_id)` even after history pruning. This preserves the `devices`/`automations` ownership boundary and recovers short disconnects without replaying old household actions, at the cost of deliberately suppressing actions after longer outages, growing a compact receipt set, and retaining a narrow unmatched-redelivery race because Automations write no receipt for every unmatched Fact.
